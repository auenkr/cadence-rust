# History decision latency follow-up (2026-11-25)

The benchmark branch started at master `76bbc723d`, which already contains
`8ea8106ca`, the History fix that advances the immediate max read level before
notifying the transfer queue. Go holds the shard write lock across both
operations. Before the Rust fix, the awakened reader could observe the old
level and defer the task until another poll.

The pre-fix [trace run](https://github.com/auenkr/cadence-rust/actions/runs/45514180239)
measured 215 of 1,010 notify-to-executor gaps over 30 ms (p95 39.97 ms).
AddDecisionTask send to RecordDecisionTaskStarted ingress was 1.01 ms at p95
and handler ingress to response conversion was 4.26 ms at p95. The original
40 ms mode was visible before Matching or the RecordDecisionTaskStarted handler.

The previous [post-fix run](https://github.com/auenkr/cadence-rust/actions/runs/45519128738)
at `8ea8106ca` ran five paired batches of 1,000 measured workflows at c1.
Counts below come directly from `successful_latency_samples_ms` with a strict
`> 60` ms cutoff. Its runner was Ubuntu 26.04.1, AMD EPYC 9V74, 4 logical CPUs
and 15 GiB RAM. Each service was limited to 1 CPU and 1,400 MiB, and MySQL was
limited to 1.5 CPUs and 1,800 MiB.

| Arm | >60 ms per repetition | Pooled >60 ms | Median workflows/s |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Go | 249, 332, 280, 155, 252 | 1,268 / 5,000 (25.36%) | 17.07 |
| Rust History | 190, 227, 184, 186, 218 | 1,005 / 5,000 (20.10%) | 21.38 |

## Queue v2 follow-up at `de5e00171080bbc6e262f1cf1bee5b0ea80a1cb1`

v2. This revision also traces when v2 receives the transfer task from its
reader (the cache or persistence) and tags batches where the limiter wait
exceeds 5 ms. The earlier [poll-rate experiment](https://github.com/auenkr/cadence-rust/actions/runs/45500331052)
did not exclude limiter waits. At its actual head `1693a0222`, the workflow
passed `DYNAMIC_CONFIG_EXTRA` only to the open-loop SDK job, which was skipped.
The paired job used the default configuration.
